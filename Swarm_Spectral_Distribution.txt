Swarm intelligence mapped onto spectral space fits well with the stochastic-cloud thinking in your Xenakis work — instead of hand-placing partials, a flock of agents self-organizes across frequency/amplitude space and you steer the emergent distribution with cohesion/separation/alignment/attractor/entropy controls. I'll build it as a 14-slider JSFX synth with a live swarm visualization (trailing dots = spectral cloud density).How it works:

- **Spectral space**: each agent lives at (log-freq, amplitude) ∈ [0,1]². Position maps directly to a sine partial's frequency and gain each control step (512 samples), then gets one-pole smoothed per-sample into `afreq`/`aamp` to kill zipper noise.
- **Boids rules**: cohesion pulls agents toward the swarm centroid, separation pushes apart anyone closer than `sep_radius`, alignment averages nearby velocities — the usual flocking triad, just running in spectral rather than physical space.
- **Attractor**: `attract_freq` places a fixed pull-point in the spectrum; raising `attract_strength` collapses the swarm into a formant-like cluster around it, lowering it lets cohesion/separation dominate for a more diffuse cloud.
- **Chaos**: literal per-agent stochastic jitter added to the force sum — a crude entropy knob in the spirit of your Shannon-entropy work, though it's just noise injection here rather than an actual entropy-measured control signal. Could extend it to measure real distribution entropy across `ax[]` and feed that back as a modulation source if you want a truer parallel to the Entropic Cluster Engine.
- **Visualization**: `@gfx` draws each agent as a dot (color = frequency position, radius = amplitude) with a trailing alpha-fade, so you can watch clustering/dispersal happen in real time — useful for finding interesting parameter regions before committing.

Things worth tuning by ear: `sep_radius` interacts strongly with `num_agents` (more agents + small radius = more collision events = busier separation force); `swarm_speed` and the control-rate interval (`block_size_ctrl = 512`, hardcoded) jointly set how fast the spectral texture evolves — lower `block_size_ctrl` for finer-grained motion at the cost of more CPU.
